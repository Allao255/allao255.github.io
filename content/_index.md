I am a final-year Electrical Engineering student (Electronics) at the São Carlos School of Engineering, University of São Paulo (EESC-USP). I am currently an engineering intern at Embraer Defense & Security.

As an undergraduate researcher at LSEC, ICMC-USP, I work on two fronts: GPS spoofing and jamming against UAVs, using SDRs to generate attack signals, and RF fingerprinting, capturing real device transmissions to build datasets for identification models.

I enjoy hacking content, tweaking hardware and measuring how things behave. Most of my projects involve software-defined radio, sub-GHz and Bluetooth hardware, RFID, and guitar pedals.
